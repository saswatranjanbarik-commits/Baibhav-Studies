# User Manual

Welcome to the Dugu Learning App! This guide will help you understand how to use the web application effectively. The features and access levels are separated based on your role: **Admin**, **Teacher**, or **Student**.

---

## 1. Admin Manual

### Overview
As an Admin, you have full control over the application. You can manage users, oversee the curriculum, track progress, and configure global settings.

### Key Features
- **User Management:**
  - Navigate to the **Manage Users** page to add, edit, or remove users (Teachers and Students).
  - Assign roles and reset passwords if necessary.
- **Syllabus & Curriculum:**
  - In the **Syllabus** section, you can add subjects, chapters, topics, and subtopics.
  - You can also upload a predefined syllabus via CSV.
- **Monitoring & Progress:**
  - Use the **Dashboard** to view global stats such as total points, topics logged, and recent activities.
  - View **Calendar** events to ensure classes and exams are scheduled properly.

---

## 2. Teacher Manual

### Overview
As a Teacher, your primary role is to manage the study plan, evaluate students, and create study materials.

### Key Features
- **Study Plan & Daily Logs:**
  - Create and manage the **Study Plan** for your classes.
  - Use the **Daily Log** to record what was taught in class. Your logs will automatically reflect in the Calendar and update the Subject Progress.
- **Study Materials & Flashcards:**
  - Go to **Flashcards** to create flashcards and quizzes for chapters or topics.
  - Access **Study Notes** and **Team Notes** to share important information and collaborative materials with students.
- **Syllabus Navigation:**
  - In the **Syllabus** view, you can see all topics and subtopics.
  - You'll find quick links to Flashcards/Quizzes and view the Activity Log associated with each topic.
- **Appreciation & Revisions:**
  - Issue **Appreciations** (points) to students for good performance.
  - Schedule **Revisions** to remind students to review specific topics.

---

## 3. Student Manual

### Overview
As a Student, you can track your study progress, complete tasks, and review materials assigned by your teachers.

### Key Features
- **Dashboard & Progress:**
  - The **Dashboard** provides an overview of your Subject Progress. It updates automatically when topics are marked as "Completed" in the Daily Log.
  - View your upcoming tasks, revisions due, and total appreciation points.
- **Daily Log:**
  - Log your self-study hours, tutoring sessions, or playtime using the **Daily Log**.
  - When you complete a topic, ensure you mark it as "Completed" so it counts towards your Subject Progress.
- **Calendar & Timetable:**
  - The **Calendar** displays your daily logs, events (like Exams or Classes), and tasks due.
  - Use the **Timetable** to stay organized with your weekly schedule.
- **Learning Materials:**
  - Click on the **Syllabus** to see what you need to study. Expand chapters/topics to view activity logs and click the link to access Flashcards & Quizzes for that section.
  - Use **Flashcards** and **Study Notes** to revise for your exams.
