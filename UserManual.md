# User Manual

Welcome to the Dugu Learning App! This guide will help you understand how to use the web application effectively. The features and access levels are separated based on your role: **Admin**, **Teacher**, or **Student**.

---

## 1. Admin Manual

### Overview
As an Admin, you have full control over the application. You can manage users, oversee the curriculum, track progress, and configure global settings.

### Key Features
- **User Management:**
  - Navigate to the **Manage Users** page to add, edit, or remove users (Teachers and Students).
  - Assign roles and reset passwords if necessary.
  - There is no public sign-up: every person must be added here before they can log in.
- **Backup:**
  - Open the **Backup** page and download a backup regularly (e.g. weekly).
  - "Restore missing items" puts back any records that disappeared, without changing existing data.
- **Syllabus & Curriculum:**
  - In the **Syllabus** section, you can add subjects, chapters, topics, and subtopics.
  - You can also upload a predefined syllabus via CSV.
- **Monitoring & Progress:**
  - Use the **Dashboard** to view global stats such as total points, topics logged, and recent activities.
  - View **Calendar** events to ensure classes and exams are scheduled properly.

## 2. Teacher Manual

### Overview
As a Teacher, your primary role is to manage the study plan, evaluate students, and create study materials.

### Key Features
- **Study Plan & Daily Logs:**
  - Create and manage the **Study Plan** for your classes.
  - Use the **Daily Log** to record what was taught in class. Your logs will automatically reflect in the Calendar and update the Subject Progress.
- **Study Materials & Flashcards:**
  - Go to **Flashcards** to create flashcards and quizzes for chapters or topics.
  - **Doubts:** open doubts appear at the top of the **Dashboard**. Press **Answer doubts**, write an answer and press **Mark resolved**.
  - **Files for a task:** use **Attach files** when creating a task, or **Attach files for the student** on any task — record audio instructions, add a PDF, photos or scans.
  - **Next Event (Dashboard):** press ✎ to change it. When it has passed, the next Calendar event is shown automatically.
  - **Homework submissions:** on **Tasks**, tap a submitted page to view it full screen (zoom, rotate, download) or press **Download PDF** for all pages in one file. Write feedback and press **Mark reviewed**.
  - **Saving space:** press **Submissions: … MB** at the top of Tasks. Use **Download & free space** (keeps a PDF on your computer, removes the pages from EduTrack, the task stays completed), or delete old completed tasks.
  - **Scanned answers in class** appear on the class board — tap to enlarge.
  - **Study Notes:** pick subject and chapter (topic is optional) and paste the link — the title is made automatically. Use ✏️ to edit and 🗑 to delete a note.
  - Access **Study Notes** and **Team Notes** to share important information and collaborative materials with students.
- **Online classes:**
  - Open **Online Classes → Schedule class**. Pick the subject/chapter, type the start in *your* local time, choose the length and whether it repeats weekly. The student sees it in their own time zone.
  - Press **Join** (from 10 minutes before). The video opens inside EduTrack; allow the camera and microphone the first time.
  - After the class press **Leave** (or **Complete**) and tick the topics covered — this adds it to the Daily Log and schedules revisions.
- **Class room windows (v1.5):**
  - The class room has four windows: **Video**, **Whiteboard**, **Questions** and **Learning** (the whiteboard starts switched off — press ✎). Use the buttons at the top right to switch each one on or off, and drag the bars between them to resize (double-click a bar to reset; ↺ resets everything). Each device remembers its own layout.
  - **Questions → Class board** is shared live with the student: read questions, **Reply**, and **Mark answered**. **My notes** is your own notepad for the class (Download saves a copy).
  - **Learning** shows the study notes, flashcards and quiz for the class topics (change the list to Chapter / Subject / Everything). **Show to class** puts a flashcard or note on the class board; **Ask the class** posts a quiz question that the student answers live — you see their answer.
  - **Layout:** only you (tutor/admin) can switch windows on/off and resize them — the student's screen follows your layout.
  - **YouTube in Present mode:** open a YouTube study note while presenting; when you play, pause or jump, the student's video does the same (sound plays on their device).
  - **PDF / Google Drive files:** the student sees the same file, but not your scrolling — use **Share screen**, pick this tab and tick **Also share tab audio**.
  - **Present a link:** in **Learning → Notes**, paste a website / YouTube / Google Drive link at the top and press **Open**. With **Present to class** on, the student sees the same page. **Save to notes** keeps it in Study Notes.
  - **Present to class:** at the top of the **Learning** window press **Present to class**. The student's Learning window now shows exactly what you open — the note, the flashcard (and when you flip it), the quiz question (and when you show the answer). Press **Stop presenting** when done. To show scrolling inside a document, use **Share screen** instead.
  - **Share screen / Chat:** buttons at the top of the class room (work for the student too). Screen sharing needs a laptop or computer.
  - **Whiteboard:** draw or write with the mouse, finger or stylus — the student sees it within a second and can draw too. Pick a tool (pen, highlighter, line, box, circle, text, eraser), colour and size; **Undo** removes your own last stroke; **+** adds a page (everyone moves to it); the bin clears the page for everyone; the download button saves the page as a picture.
  - The class board stays with the class: open **Class board** on the class card later to review the questions.
- **Marking topics completed:**
  - On the **Syllabus** page, use **Mark topics completed** to tick off everything already covered in class (you can tick whole chapters or all Term-1 chapters at once), or use **✓ Mark completed** on a single topic. **Unmark** undoes it.
- **Syllabus Navigation:**
  - In the **Syllabus** view, you can see all topics and subtopics.
  - You'll find quick links to Flashcards/Quizzes and view the Activity Log associated with each topic.
- **Appreciation & Revisions:**
  - Issue **Appreciations** (points) to students for good performance.
  - Schedule **Revisions** to remind students to review specific topics.

## 3. Student Manual

### Overview
As a Student, you can track your study progress, complete tasks, and review materials assigned by your teachers.

### Key Features
- **Dashboard & Progress:**
  - The **Dashboard** provides an overview of your Subject Progress. It updates automatically when topics are marked as "Completed" in the Daily Log.
  - View your upcoming tasks, revisions due, and total appreciation points.
- **Daily Log:**
  - Log your self-study hours, tutoring sessions, or playtime using the **Daily Log**.
  - When you complete a topic, ensure you mark it as "Completed" so it counts towards your Subject Progress.
- **Calendar & Timetable:**
  - The **Calendar** displays your daily logs, events (like Exams or Classes), and tasks due.
  - Use the **Timetable** to stay organized with your weekly schedule.
- **Learning Materials:**
  - Click on the **Syllabus** to see what you need to study. Expand chapters/topics to view activity logs and click the link to access Flashcards & Quizzes for that section.
  - Use **Flashcards** and **Study Notes** to revise for your exams.
- **Daily Plan & Log:** in the morning press **Plan a session** for each thing you will do — choose the type (Self study, Tutor class, School, Play, Activity), then the subject (or the play/activity) and the time. When you finish one, press **✓ Done** (or **Log details…** to add notes) — it goes into your Daily Log automatically.
- **Doubts:** on **Daily Plan & Log**, press **Ask a doubt**, choose the subject and write (or 🎤 speak) your doubt. Your tutor sees it on the Dashboard and writes the answer under it. If you log a session as **Have Doubt**, you can write the doubt right there.
- **Submitting homework:** under each task choose **Scan**, **Photos**, **PDF**, **Audio** (record your answer) or **Link**. Files from your tutor (for example audio instructions) appear under the task as **From the tutor**.
- **Scanning tips:** on **Tasks**, press **Scan & submit**, take a photo of each page (lay it flat on a darker table, good light). The app finds the page edges and straightens it — use **Adjust corners** if it missed a corner. Press **Submit** and wait for **✓ Submitted and saved**. Use **Add more pages** if you forgot one. Your tutor's feedback appears under the task.
- **In class:** to show your written answer, press the 📷 button next to Send on the class board, take the photo and press **Send to class** — your tutor sees it straight away. Press **Reply** on the tutor's question first to attach it as your answer.
- **Dictation (speak instead of typing):** tap into a text box, press the round 🎤 button on the right and speak. Say "full stop", "comma", "question mark" or "new line" for punctuation. Press it again to stop. (Your tablet keyboard's own 🎤 also works.)
- **Online classes:**
  - Press **Join** on **Online Classes** (from 10 minutes before) and allow the camera and microphone.
  - In class, type questions or comments in the **Questions** window — your tutor sees them straight away. Use **My notes** for your own notes.
  - Open notes, flashcards or quiz questions for the lesson in the **Learning** window.
  - When your tutor presents, your Learning window shows what they show. Press **Browse myself** to look around on your own, and **Follow** to go back.
  - Use **Chat** at the top for the video chat. **Share screen** works when you join from a laptop/computer (not from an Android tablet).
  - When your tutor uses the **Whiteboard** (✎ button), you can write on it too — e.g. show your working. Download a page to keep it. Switch windows on/off with the buttons at the top, and drag the bars to make a window bigger.
